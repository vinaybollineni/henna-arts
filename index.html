Henna Art That Celebrates Your Moments

Welcome to Meghna's Henna & Mehndi Art, where each design is a unique creation crafted with love and precision. Serving Liberty Hill, Leander, Georgetown, Round Rock, Austin and surrounding areas, I specialize in creating elegant mehndi art using my own 100% organic homemade henna paste for a luxurious experience.

My designs are more than beautiful patterns — they tell individual stories and celebrate special moments. Whether it's your wedding mehndi, a festive celebration, or a personal milestone, I treat each client like family.

100% Organic Homemade Henna

Freshly prepared for every application

At Meghna's Henna & Mehndi Art, I take pride in preparing my own organic henna paste from scratch using only the finest natural ingredients. Unlike pre-made commercial henna, my homemade recipe ensures the richest, darkest stain and the safest experience for your skin.

100% Natural Organic Ingredients

Pure organic henna powder, essential oils, lemon juice — no chemicals or additives

Freshly Prepared for You

Each batch made fresh to ensure optimal color, consistency, and quality

Rich, Dark Stain

My special organic recipe creates a deep, long-lasting burgundy-brown color

Safe for All Skin Types

Gentle, hypoallergenic organic formula safe for sensitive skin, pregnant women, and children

No Black Henna

Never contains PPD or harmful chemicals found in "black henna"

"I personally prepare every batch of organic henna and mehndi paste with love and care, using a traditional recipe passed down and perfected over time. Your safety and satisfaction are my top priorities!"
