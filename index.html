Premium Organic Homemade Henna & Mehndi

At Meghna's Henna & Mehndi Art, I take pride in preparing my own organic henna paste from scratch using only the finest natural ingredients for all my applications. Unlike pre-made commercial henna, my homemade recipe ensures the richest, darkest stain and the safest experience for your skin.

100% Natural Organic Ingredients

Pure organic henna powder, essential oils, lemon juice, and natural ingredients – no chemicals or additives

Freshly Prepared for You

Each batch is made fresh to ensure optimal color, consistency, and quality for every client

Rich, Dark Stain

My special organic recipe creates a deep, long-lasting burgundy-brown color that develops beautifully

Safe for All Skin Types

Gentle, hypoallergenic organic formula safe for sensitive skin, pregnant women, and children

Perfect Consistency

Carefully balanced texture allows for intricate, detailed designs that flow beautifully

No Black Henna

Never contains PPD or harmful chemicals found in "black henna" that can cause severe reactions
